# ClaimPilot pilot: your task (one page)

You are acting as an insurance claims adjuster reviewing **synthetic** auto-collision claims. No
real people or claims are involved.

**What to do.** Sign in, open **My tasks**, and work through the 12 claims in order. For each,
decide **Approve**, **Deny** or **Escalate** and press the decision button. You will see two kinds
of claim:

- **With the AI assistant:** the page shows a recommendation, the policy clauses it relies on
  (quoted in full), and the fields it read. Check it against the documents; do not just click.
  Choosing something different from the recommendation needs a reason from a short list.
  Fields marked "read from scan" must be ticked as matching the page before you can decide.
- **Manual:** only the documents and the policy wording. There is no AI panel.

**What we measure.** The time from when you first open a claim to when you submit, your decisions
and override reasons, and your answers on the feedback form. We are measuring the tool, not you.

**Please.**
- Work the claims in order, one at a time, in one sitting if you can (about 60 to 90 minutes).
- Don't look things up elsewhere or discuss claims with other testers until everyone has finished.
- If you are interrupted, just carry on; interrupted sessions are set aside automatically.
- The first claim is a warm-up and is not timed in the results.
- Don't type names or personal details in notes or feedback.
- When you finish, fill in the short **Feedback** form (4 questions).

Thank you.
