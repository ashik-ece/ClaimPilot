# 15-minute onboarding script

1. **(2 min) Context.** Say what ClaimPilot is: it drafts a cited recommendation for a claims
   adjuster; the adjuster decides. Everything is synthetic. Say what is measured (time per claim,
   decisions, override reasons, feedback) and that the tool is being tested, not the tester.
2. **(3 min) Walk-through, assisted.** Open a practice claim that is not in the pool. Show: the
   documents on the left; the recommendation and the clauses it cites on the right; the flags
   ("look harder, not a finding"); the fields tagged "read from scan" and why they must be checked
   against the page.
3. **(2 min) Deciding.** Choose a different outcome than recommended to show the reason list. Show
   that the note is optional except for "other".
4. **(2 min) Manual mode.** Show a manual claim: documents plus the policy, no AI panel.
5. **(1 min) The clock.** It starts when you first open a claim and stops when you submit.
   Reopening does not restart it.
6. **(3 min) Their turn.** The tester opens **My tasks** and does the first claim with you watching,
   without help unless something is broken.
7. **(2 min) Questions, then leave them to work alone.**

Do not coach on which outcome is right. Note down anything confusing for the retrospective.
